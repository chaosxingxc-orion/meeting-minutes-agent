# E-CHUNK-RETRIEVAL verdict

The registered 7,145-call experiment completed and returned
`CHUNK-RETRIEVAL-NOT-REACHED`. Structural replay and convergence passed, but the routed
speaker arm reduced consistency by 6.42 points versus bare, won against bare in 0/4
meetings and against the equal-cardinality deranged arm in only 1/4 meetings. It also
failed worst-speaker and unsupported-activation safety gates.

This is a convergent self-reinforcement result, not a proof that all per-chunk retrieval
is impossible. The previous same-chunk hypothesis supplies spelling proposed by the same
model, so repeated output is not independent corrective evidence. The admissible next
step is a new zero-model audit of leave-one-chunk-out, cross-occurrence evidence. No new
model flight, threshold tuning, GRPO, GEPA, EM update, or multimodal injection is admitted
by this verdict.
