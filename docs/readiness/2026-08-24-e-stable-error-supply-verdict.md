# E-STABLE-ERROR-SUPPLY verdict

Decision: **`STABLE-ERROR-SUPPLY-PRESENT-ANCHOR-LIMITED`**.

The complete 1,429-call Pass-0 produced 13 registered stable-wrong groups across all
four meetings, passing the error-supply gates. It produced zero ticker-anchored
stable-wrong groups, failing both legal-anchor gates. No Pass1 or optimization loop is
authorized by this result.

The evidence supports the meeting-level clustering mechanism: repeated exact-form errors
can be stable across independently processed short turns. It does not yet support a
professional-term correction mechanism. A labelled post-hoc diagnostic found that 9 of
13 strict errors are separator-only abbreviation variants, leaving at most four
non-separator candidates. This diagnostic cannot change the registered decision, but it
prevents interpreting the 13 groups as 13 semantic term errors.

A second post-hoc mechanism signal is concentrated output-language drift: 125/450 replies
in meeting `4430051` contained non-ASCII text and no ASCII letters, versus two such replies
across the other 979 calls. Meeting language is a legal, independent anchor and may justify
a separately registered whole-meeting language-controllability experiment. It is not a
speaker-term Pass1 and must not be used to claim the original optimization objective.
