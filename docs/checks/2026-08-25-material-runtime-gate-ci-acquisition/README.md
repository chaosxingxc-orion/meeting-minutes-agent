PDF or HTML structure, raw SHA-256 provenance, and at least eight deterministic
candidate surfaces. The snapshot contains six sources, 54 pages, and 1,403 raw

excluded.

amendments before their respective successful acquisitions. No meeting was
replaced and no reference, Pass0, retrieval score, or outcome informed those
changes. One zero-byte failed Ferrari placeholder was verified and removed;
all admitted bytes remain recoverable from the frozen URLs.

calls, and Omni calls. The pass permits only registration of the missing Pass0
flight; it does not establish semantic-gate or transcription benefit.
